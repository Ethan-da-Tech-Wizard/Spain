# Spain: Minecraft Implementation Plan

Updated October 9, 2026. The current test uses Minecraft Java 26.3, a Fabric flight mod and an adventure data pack.

## Implemented test

- Normal chunk-loaded Earth, Harborlight city, native Minecraft wildlife/hostiles and a mixed Spore/Minecraft appearance palette.
- Thirteen residents, including a rocket scientist and six station workers, with native right-click dialogue.
- Introductory city → scientist → rocket → station story; all building and travel also work without NPCs.
- Rocket creator with imported hulls/engines, wings, cockpits, stretch, preview rotation and undo; unrestricted part additions.
- Block-sculpture capture, cockpit-centered free flight, chase camera, independent board/land/recall and in-flight quick travel.
- Altitude-based planet/orbit transitions, forgiving support physics and water buoyancy.
- Spacesuit and finite underwater breath.
- Three colony destinations and a persistent cooperation quest.
- Local Melty/Spore conversion, avatar selection and captured creature appearances.
- Real Minecraft server verification, resource/download status checks, library repair and a managed Prism installer.

## Following milestones

1. Expand the creator into direct part manipulation, continuous transforms, better sculpting and ship collision during piloted movement.
2. Larger terrain and city generation for the colony planets; smoother planetary transition presentation.
3. More Spore parts/creations and distinct animated creature rigs. Keep native Minecraft texel density and block materials throughout.
4. Connect the tested population progression model to loaded/visited settlements. Begin with babies; gate creature → tribe → city → space on survival, learning and cooperation rather than a global timer.
5. Lucy's reactive guidance, companions, faction relationships and multiplayer friendship quests.
6. The true final invasion becomes a negotiated coalition through trust, evidence, fulfilled promises and friendship. Gathering and combat remain choices; killing is never required for the final diplomatic ending.

The first station welcome completes the current test, not the full campaign. The player can continue exploring afterward. The latest camera revision is awaiting visual verification on a manual launch; automated work must not open the game.
